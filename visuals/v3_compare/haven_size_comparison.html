Haven on the ear: v2, v3 and a typical hearing aid

The whole device each time: the pod behind the ear, the wire over the top, and the piece in the ear. Same ear, same camera, so only the hardware changes. v3 is the design being worked on now. Pictures taken from the 3D explorer on 14 Nov 2026. Nothing here has been built.

The v3 in-ear piece, first draft

A small capsule that holds only the speaker and carries the sealing tip. The microphone moves to the pod, so there is no circuit board in the ear and the wire has two strands. Cut through the middle, it looks like this:

Haven v2 in-ear pieceHaven v3 in-ear piece (draft)
Hard part15 × 16 × 22 mm7.0 mm across, 13.2 mm long with nozzle
Printed weightabout 1.7 gabout 0.5 g with the speaker (estimate)
Insidespeaker, microphone, circuit boardspeaker and one small resistor

The capsule is sized from the speaker maker's drawing. The maker lists 104 dB at 1 kHz for 2 mW in a sealed ear simulator, which is more than Haven needs. At the audio chip's full power it would be far too loud (about 118 dB by calculation), so a small resistor is soldered at the speaker as a fixed limit that software cannot remove. The 104 dB is their figure in their test fixture, not a measurement in this housing, and no real speaker has been bought or measured yet. The ear tip in the drawing is a generic shape.

What to keep in mind

  • The v2 pod in the pictures is the current v2 shell file at its real size (it was drawn 2 mm too thin before 14 Nov).
  • The typical aid in the pictures is a generic shape built from published sizes, not a real product. The numbers in the table are from one maker's spec sheet (Audicus Omni) and the weight from another (Miracle-Ear).
  • The v3 in-ear piece in the pictures is the first draft shown in the drawing above. Because Haven has to seal the ear to protect it, its tip will always be fuller than a hearing aid's open dome.
  • All weights and battery lives for Haven are estimates from datasheets. Nothing has been measured or worn.

To turn the models yourself: open the explorer on the Haven website and pick "Behind-the-ear" (that is v2), "Haven v3 slim" or "Typical hearing aid" under Shape concept.
