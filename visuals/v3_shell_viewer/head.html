What you are looking at

  • Tray (blue): the half against the head. The battery lies in it.
  • Lid (orange): the half facing out. It covers the radio module and the chips. The small hole is for the microphone.
  • Board (green) with the radio module (grey) on top and the battery (silver) underneath.
  • Spring pins (gold): the charging contacts. Drawn at the real part's size: 2.0 mm base, 1.5 mm barrel, 4.5 mm tall. They run from the board, past the end of the battery, and out through the two holes on the head side.
  • The hole at the top end is where the two speaker wires leave for the ear.

Not settled yet

  • The inside parts are plain blocks showing the space each one needs, not the real shapes.
  • The spring pin shown is 4.5 mm tall, so its tip stands 0.5 mm out of the face that rests against the head. A 4.0 mm version would sit flush. The length gets picked once the battery is measured.
  • The battery's real size can differ from its listing by up to 0.5 mm in thickness. There is no spare room for that.
  • Hole positions follow the board drawing. Left or right ear has not been checked.
  • No latch, seal or magnet pocket. The two halves just slip together.

This shell is for judging size and feel on a test print. It is not the final enclosure.
